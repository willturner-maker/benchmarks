  • Tier 1 baseline (17h → 17h2): one scratch project, F2 arming ARM-CHECKED pre-dispatch — pass 1 20/21, pass 2 21/21: the new working ceiling. ~57–58 tool-name-mangling errors per pass recorded as friction, not failures.

  • Tier 3 friction wave (18k, 42 sessions, two arms): the rule does not ship. Pre-committed ship rule applied as written: (a) passed vacuously; (b) failed — arm-B T2r5 was a degenerate repetition collapse (71/72 calls errored in a mangled-name loop, davinci-resolvetime ×23 over 124 s) that violated the rule rather than following it; its final report honestly admitted the collapse. Pass axis (secondary): 40/42 verified, 42/42 honest.

5 · Tier 4 — the safe path is invisible 18l · F3 · n=6

F3's mechanism was pinned same-day before any agent ran: the same ripple insert has two legal paths with opposite physics — community ripple_insert/move_clips rebuild shifted items (destroying grade+link; the restore pass deliberately excludes grades/transitions/links), while the built-in bridge preserves ids, CDLs byte-for-byte, and links. 6 · What the arc stands for

  • Trap physics is build-specific: loss sets re-armed on 5.3.3; 5.3.4's transition was cleared by full-tree byte diff (two VERSION strings) — the next behavioral release re-runs rearm_trap-style probes before any grading.
