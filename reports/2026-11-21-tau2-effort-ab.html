4 · Incident, disclosed the tool caught its own operator

The first summary after the wave silently scored airline and telecom off the medium wave's sims: summarize.py's fallback glob (wave-*-{dom}) matched both waves and took the last match — airline printed medium's numbers verbatim. It was caught within minutes because the tool's own upstream re-derivation check disagreed (0.887/0.833 printed by τ² vs 0.8667/0.8200 re-derived — a gap that must be zero on identical records). Same-commit fix: the fallback is scoped to wave-<wave-dir>-<dom> with a loud warning on any multi-match; post-fix, raw-sims and rows re-derivations agree exactly, and the published-wave stats gate still passes byte-identically.
