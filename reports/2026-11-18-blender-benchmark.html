Blender Benchmark — Fixture-Class Debut

A new suite measuring Cycles render throughput on a custom scene — the brief being “a pretty woman in a red dress sitting at a table with a computer and a lamp.” The official Blender Benchmark Launcher has no supported custom-scene path, so this harness reproduces its samples-per-minute metric directly: fixed 1080p scene, METAL device, denoising off, a 64/128/256 spp sweep, every result graded by independent pixel read-backs. The woman herself is a generated fixture (Meshy via MCP-for-Blender Premium), and fixture class is part of the run identity — stand-in and generated scores are separate series, never compared.

2 · Fixture provenance Meshy · MCP-for-Blender Premium

The woman is a generated asset: MCP-for-Blender's Premium pipeline (a plan license — no BYOK generator keys). Three high-quality generations: two were discarded because the backend baked a table+laptop into the subject despite explicit “no furniture” prompts; the third (“collectible figurine… sitting cross-legged… no furniture”) came back subject-only — red dress, cross-legged, 1.10M faces, origin at floor. The bench scene re-scales, grounds and orients it, and keeps its own procedural low table, hinged laptop and architect lamp staged around her. The 38 MB GLB stays out of git (SHA-256 pinned into every run record); full prompt/job/license details live in fixture/PROVENANCE.json. Premium props were tried too (table, lamp) and rejected — untextured block/cone output — so the generated asset is the woman only.

4 · A voided run integrity read-back earned its keep

The first --no-fixture final sealed at 4,167 — and its own scene_line confessed: 606,829 verts on a run that should have had 6,618. The flag was parsed but never propagated to the scene build, and the recorder stamped the fixture SHA whenever the GLB merely existed. The run was voided and deleted. The pipeline hardened the same day: the builder prints fixture=glb|none in SCENE_OK, run_bench aborts on any mismatch with intent, and records carry an explicit fixture_class field. The re-run stand-in landed 7,581 vs 7,589 from earlier the same day — 0.1 % reproducibility on the procedural class.
