Reading the wave honestly

· Plateau shift is scatter, not a claimed shape change. Peak moved back to w8 (522.7) from w12 (524.8) and the plateau mean sits ~4% lower — inside the instance-to-instance variation the Nov 10 all-zeros-vs-demotes lesson warned about.
· Demote path engaged wider: this wave demoted at w6/w10/w12 (+382/+645/+97) vs w10-only on Nov 11, and one waiting_memory hold at w10 (same width as Nov 11; 1.6.0 held at w12). Suspension stop-rule never fired anywhere.
· Single-tenant caveat: the bench engine also served this session's Claude traffic during the run. All per-round suspension deltas were 0 (the lifetime counter's 16 pre-date the wave), so stage numbers hold — but strict single-tenant hygiene was technically broken.
· Resident KV budget is instance-variable: 131,072 reported now vs 139,264 in the baseline doc.
