2 · Reading the deltas honestly what this does and does not prove

No epoch effect. The only cross-epoch confound flagged in the same-seed pair was telecom running high; the replay puts telecom pass^1 at 0.8713 against the old epoch's 0.8596 — indistinguishable. Aggregate movement (≤1.3 pts) is what the known flip rates predict for independent three-trial waves.

Retail moves most (−5.4/−7.7/−8.1) but that is retail being retail: 29 of 74 tasks disagree across three trials in this very wave, and retail has held the widest trial-disagreement share in every wave here. Single-wave domain deltas of this size carry no signal; the aggregate does.

Consequence: the published headline (0.8501 / 0.7591 / 0.6933) stands unadjusted, and no epoch correction will be applied to future waves.
