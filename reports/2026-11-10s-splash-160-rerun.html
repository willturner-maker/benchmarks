Method & deviations

Probe stage crashed, then re-ran

The committed engine_probe.py appends to a hardcoded /tmp dir that had been cleaned, and never mkdirs — FileNotFoundError on its first emit. Re-ran standalone after the ladder/KV/agentic stages with the engine idle; probe numbers are post-suite, not interleaved. (The chain log also misreported the exit code: $(date) before rc=$? in zsh.)

Combined entry point, first full run

The baseline tables came from the per-method originals; this is the first end-to-end ladder_kv.py pass. Its KV stage reports gauge peaks in raw pages (×32 = tokens) where the baseline table printed tokens — cross-compare at the ×32 factor.

Conditions identical to baseline rows

Engine 1.6.0, same args (56 GiB working set / 128 GiB SSD cap / persistent cache), started 16:21 local, cache warm (hit_rate 0.9920 at suite start), never restarted. Operator session live through the whole suite. Agentic driver used the run-2 conditions: --strict-mcp-config, per-task dirs, curl-only/Read-only allowlists, fixture seeded into S4 by the operator.
