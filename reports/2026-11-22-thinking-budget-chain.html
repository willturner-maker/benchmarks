The thinking-budget A/B could not bind — so it publishes the noise floor instead

Three waves of the same 238 tasks, one per engine --thinking-token-budget (8192, 65536, and the pinned 32,768 relaunched last as a same-session control). The flag was set differently three times and changed nothing: the harness caps completion at max_tokens 4,096, and thinking tokens count inside that cap, so every arm's budget sat above the ceiling the model was allowed to reach. What is left is the measurement the suite actually needed — how much τ² moves when nothing is varied: pass^1 spreads 3.8 points and 17.6–19.7 % of tasks flip, with no pair separable by an exact sign test.

3 · Why the arms were inert — arithmetic, not measurement the correction

On this stack, reasoning tokens are billed inside completion_tokens. Measured directly the same day: max_tokens=16 → completion_tokens=16 with reasoning_tokens=16 and content=null; the same prompt at 200 answered correctly with 23 reasoning tokens. So completion ≤ 4,096 always, and an engine budget above 4,096 can never be the binding constraint.   • queued never exceeded 1: a single-tenant τ² wave does not queue, which is why the --queue-size 64 stack move is invisible to this suite in both directions.
  • The wave's own --max-concurrency 3 shows up in the engine's counters: decode width b3 dominates by an order of magnitude and b4 is never reached, so maximum_batch_width: 4 is never filled here.
  • Memory-path engagement: spill 0, suspensions 0, and kv_demotions +3,164 / +2,595 — persistent-cache churn without pressure.
  • The 65536 arm's engine samples are excluded, not averaged in. Its directory was re-entered by a stalled re-run: 30 of 552 samples show live requests, queued max 32, and max_new_tokens of 8 / 32,768 where the harness declares 4,096 — that is foreign traffic. Its outcome data is verified intact; only its console and engine evidence degraded.

5 · Provenance: what went wrong, stated in the record

  • Five chain launches. The first failed its own argv guard because the guard looked at the engine child, whose argv carries no options at all — the supervisor carries them. Two more launches aborted on the engine-idle guard rather than killing a busy engine. The last one stalled on τ²'s interactive "resume the run? (y/n)" prompt for 84 minutes with the engine idle, which is how the runner got hardened: </dev/null stdin, a pre-flight check on --save-to, a completed-arm skip guard, and a stall watchdog that watches engine batch counters rather than τ²'s CPU.
  • Four analyzer bugs are published in the record because each would have printed plausible numbers: reward lives under reward_info.reward; τ² task ids are numeric per domain, so keying on task_id alone merged airline task 3 with retail task 3; the crowd-out detector counted tool-call turns as truncations; and deriving the harness cap from engine samples is wrong for a contaminated arm.
  • One operator-caused tenancy sample inside the 65536 window is recorded rather than suppressed (active 36 / queued 32 / decoding 0, spill/demote/suspensions 0). The arm was characterised, not voided.
  • Every number on this page was diffed against arm_readout.json programmatically (74 checks). One FAIL in that check was the checker, not the data — it subtracted an airline-only count.
