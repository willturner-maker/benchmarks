2 · The correction: instability is a rate, not a roster read 19a's "45 flips" this way now

Which tasks flipped, wave to wave
238 tasks, five trials total across the two waves (2 + 3)

Rates replicate: ~19 % of tasks flip within 2 trials, 27 % within 3, 36 % across all five. The roster does not: only 24 of 19a's 45 unstable tasks flip again; 62 flip in exactly one of the two waves' views. 152 tasks (64 %) never flip in five tries. A per-task "unstable" label now needs several passes before it gets published — the one surviving label is retail/90, unstable in every pass it has ever appeared in (3/5 lifetime).

The share attributable to engine nondeterminism still needs the same-seed repeat, which remains the open τ² experiment.

4 · Engine footnote, and the run's own incidents disclosed, not smoothed

τ² calls are shallow — max 19,035 tok = 0.122× KV budget. But the retail window's one foreign sample was the operator's own session at 133,473 tokens: alone it pushed pages_active to 0.86× of the resident pool and moved kv_demotions +5,301 — an unplanned live corroboration of the same engine's tenancy A/B. Incidents from getting 714 sims done: a relative-path wrapper bug (died pre-flight, zero sims), a zsh word-splitting failure on retail's 74 ids (died at task resolution), one killed-and-quarantined retail attempt (1 sim), and a sampler truncation by the continuation — which is why the airline/telecom windows are tenancy-by-protocol while retail's is tenancy-by-samples.
