What this corrects: 19a/pass^3 said "fresh user-simulator seed per trial, so flips are user-sampling variance." With the seed fixed the transcripts still all diverge — at temperature 0 there is barely any "resampling" left; the seed swap contributes roughly the remaining ~12 points of the 3-trial 27.3 %. "User-first" survives as where the engine's divergence surfaces first (a new open question: why user turns ~3:1), not as the cause. Rule 9 now carries the mechanism: micro-divergence is near-universal, reward flips are a per-task lottery — which is exactly why rates replicate and rosters don't.
